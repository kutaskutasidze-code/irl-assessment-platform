## 🎯 IRL Alignment

All questions now follow the **KTH Innovation Readiness Level™** framework:

### Scoring Philosophy
| Score | IRL Level | Maturity Stage | Description |
|-------|-----------|----------------|-------------|
| 0 | 1-2 | Idea/Early | Not started or very early stage |
| 2 | 3-4 | Concept/Prototype | Basic implementation, not validated |
| 3.5 | 5-6 | Validation | Tested in relevant environment |
| 4.5 | 7-8 | Demonstration/Pilot | Proven in operational setting |
| 5 | 9 | Scale/Market Leader | Industry-leading performance |

## 💡 Key Insights from Enhancement Process

### What Made These Questions Better:

1. **Real KPIs**: Questions use actual metrics that investors and corporates evaluate
   - AI/ML: MLOps maturity, not just "do you have ML?"
   - HealthTech: CPT codes and FDA pathways, not just "are you compliant?"
   - FinTech: Transaction success rates and regulatory audit history

2. **Progressive Maturity**: Scoring reflects actual industry progression
   - 0 = Not started (IRL 1-2)
   - 2 = Basic implementation (IRL 3-4)
   - 3.5 = Validated (IRL 5-6)
   - 4.5 = Production-proven (IRL 7-8)
   - 5 = Industry-leading (IRL 9)

3. **Regulatory Alignment**: Questions reference actual compliance requirements
   - AI/ML: ISO 42001, bias testing standards
   - HealthTech: HIPAA SRA, BAAs, FDA 510(k)
   - FinTech: SOC 2 Type II, PCI-DSS, money transmitter licenses

4. **Contextual Help**: Every question explains WHY it matters and what good looks like
   - Benchmarks provided (e.g., "99.9% uptime is industry standard")
   - Acronyms explained (e.g., "SHAP/LIME explanations")
   - Business impact clarified (e.g., "Required for reimbursement")
